IN-GAME HUD — SAME PRIMITIVES AS THE MENUS

Every panel here is drawn with the primitives GenShell.cpp already uses: chamfered at top-left and bottom-right only, dark navy gradient with the light wash along the top edge, blue border at half alpha, the gold accent bar along the top and gold diagonals at the two cut corners, layered shadow below. Buttons are the same button faces — blue at rest, gold border and glow on hover, gold for primary actions. Text is bold, letter-spaced, gold for what matters, blue for what is in use.

What is faithful to the menu style: the two-corner chamfer, the top gold accent bar, the gold corner diagonals, the panel gradient + top sheen, the half-alpha blue border, the shadow stack, the button faces with their hover glow, the gold primary button, the sunk insets, the thin dividers, letter-spaced bold text. What the HUD adds on top of a menu screen: the radar blips and the in-world health bar / selection ring, which live over the world rather than inside a panel — those stay plain lines, since a menu panel cannot sit over a unit.
